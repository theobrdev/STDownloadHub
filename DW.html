Slendytubbies: Dipsy's Workshop, or officially shortened to Dipsy's Workshop (acronymed to DW), is a spin-off game in the Slendytubbies Series developed and published by ZeoWorks that was released on January 2, 2015 as a New Year's gift to the ZeoWorks Community. Nevertheless, this was the first side installment of the saga — marking it as non-canon from the start.

Dipsy's Workshop is the first Slendytubbies game to be available on mobile platforms rather than just being exclusive on computers — although Dipsy's Workshop was also playable via GameShed through Adobe Flash Player and Game Jolt through Unity WebPlayer, there was no download link for the game on personal computers. As of now, Slendytubbies: Dipsy's Workshop is taken away from all sides of ZeoWorks most likely due to copyright reasons, however, irrelevance may have been another factor as well. It is important to note that copyright issues probably only had effect on Mobile platforms, whereas for GameShed, the whole site got discontinued in 2019 (terminating all games that it once had as a result), and the game was archived on Game Jolt.

Given the fact there are no official ways to play Slendytubbies: Dipsy's Workshop anymore, alternative download links exist via other digital application distributors — the following list is compiled with links that the Slendytubbies Wiki views as safe to install Dipsy's Workshop:

Description

Slendytubbies: Dipsy's Workshop in its foundations is a video game clone of Fruit Ninja, thereby it would be logically to assume that the former uses the same game mechanics as the latter. The player controls Lake Dipsy, and the overall objective of the game is to slice as many teletubbies as possible. Dipsy's Workshop has two game modes on its disposal — Classic and Timed.

Whenever a play starts, there is a three (3) second countdown after which the message “GO” pops up; the first object is thrown a little before the aforementioned message. If the player manages to split three or more (≥ 3) teletubbies in a quick attack, they are going to get a Combo Bonus depending on the amount of teletubbies they cut in that short period of time multiplied by hundred (× 100); there is no limit on how many teletubbies can get comboed, nonetheless, triple, quadruple, and quintuple combos seem to be the most common. Everytime when a higher score than the current record is accomplished, the message “New record!” appears on the screen. In contrast to the popular belief, Dipsy's Workshop is not a video game in which plays last endlessly — all plays last approximately up to five minutes and thirty seconds (≈ 5:30) no matter what game mode was chosen — once the last launched object gets sliced or ignored, it will result in a Game Over.

Classic

The objective of Classic Mode is to massacre as many teletubbies as possible — up to three chances are given to miss, and when a Tubby Custard is striked, it is an instant Game Over. When a Cyan Teletubby is cut, it makes the whole play slow down by one half (½) of its speed for six (6) seconds, and it does not grant any points (0) whatsoever — ignoring a Cyan Tubby does not cost a life. As it is in this game mode where the player can achieve the highest record overall in the entire video game, they should focus on doing combo dashes whenever probable — waiting to attack until a teletubby is about to fall off from the screen makes it the best time to strike because multiple others will be present at that moment.

Timed

The complete experience on Timed Mode revolves around a sixty (60) second timer in which the player has to kill as many teletubbies as feasible. There is no worry for lives howsoever as the penalty for missing a teletubby is non-existent in this mode, nonetheless, hitting a bowl of custard gives a negative five hundred (−500) point reduction to the score — to prevent any negative points, the player should listen carefully for the sound that is heard whenever a Teletubby Custard is launched to be prepared. An unique property of the Cyan Teletubby on Timed Mode is that they even slow the clock down, adding three (3) more seconds to the process; essentially, the timer can be extended as much as twenty times, increasing its total value close to two (2) minutes.

Teletubbies

Slendytubbies: Dipsy's Workshop features seven teletubbies, five of which that were encountered in the series previously — the White Teletubby, Tinky Winky, Dipsy, Laa-Laa, and Po, as well as two other, which are the first individuals introduced beyond the main four and the White Tubby in the saga — the Brown Teletubby and the Cyan Teletubby, although being non-canon. All of them are a reference to the Fruit from Fruit Ninja, which grant individual point values when sliced — the Cyan Tubby is the representation of the Freeze Banana.

Their main characteristic is that none of the tubbies display an antenna on top of their heads, however, all of them retain their television screens, and also they are all stuck in A-pose. Their bodies will always split in half between the upper and lower part of their figure no matter from what angle they were cut, and only the upper part of the body bleeds. One may notice after closer inspection that both parts of the bodies have a red square within them instead of internal organs — the squares are called “Quads”.

It is a believable statement that the Brown Tubby and Cyan Tubby are actually the Cave Tubby and the Yeti Tubby due the amount of similarities connected to them, or they could just be inspirations — this may even be a coincidence. Interestingly, Lake Dipsy is the only infected to be presented in the game, even though he can only be seen in the Main Menu. Ambiguously, Dipsy is also portrayed as a sliceable teletubby in-game defining the existence between the two as an enigma.

Tubby Custard

As all Slendytubbies games require some kind of danger, bowls of Teletubby Custard are the only obstacle in Dipsy's Workshop. Tubby Custards are a reference to the Bombs from Fruit Ninja — they are extremely oversized in this game making them a serious threat — whenever they are launched, an audio file plays, thus alerting the player. An original attribute of theirs is the fact that multiple bowls of custard can sometimes be thrown at once on different sides of the background. The size of the custards used to be even larger in V1.0 of Slendytubbies: Dipsy's Workshop.

Trivia

General

  • Slendytubbies: Dipsy's Workshop is…
    • The third game of the series — released on January 2, 2015 as a New Year's gift to the community.
    • Apparently, the sole spin-off game of the Slendytubbies series.
    • Also, the very first Slendytubbies video game ever given the non-canon status.
    • The earliest ZeoWorks project put on Mobile platforms.
    • Taken away from all sides of ZeoWorks, and it is the only installment of the series to be terminated in this way.
    • One of the four video games playable on mobile devices, together with Slendytubbies 2D, Slendytubbies: Anniversary Edition, and Slendytubbies III, although later being removed.
    • One of the four games to be formerly available on GameShed, along with Slendytubbies, Slendytubbies II, and Slendytubbies Campaign DEMO.
    • Exclusive to Singleplayer — Dipsy's Workshop is the sole installment of the series to have this trait.
    • The game with the least amount out of Updates in its history — marking at barely two versions — V1 and V1.5.
    • Presumably, the only installment of the saga to feature no Easter Eggs and the slightest quantity of Scrapped Content.
  • Dipsy's Workshop was created in Unity.
  • The project is not available for iOS devices.
  • Dipsy is the sole character of the saga to have his own video game.
    • Ironically, he is only shown on the Main Menu with minor presence in the gameplay.
    • Like in Slendytubbies, there is one single corrupted individual in the whole game — this time being Dipsy in his second phase.
  • Although plays do not happen on traditional Maps, which is a great contrast to all of Slendytubbies, the only location that appears in some shape or form is Teletubby Land — detectable as the background of the Main Menu where the front entrance of Tubbytronic Superdome is represented.
  • Slendytubbies: Dipsy's Workshop utilizes seven fonts in its gameplay, which is out of all Slendytubbies games, the most amount of fonts ever to be made use of — noted as follows:

In-Game

  • Dipsy's Workshop operates on the ratio of 2∶1.
  • The gameplay is based upon Fruit Ninja.
  • The player plays as Lake Dipsy.
  • Slendytubbies: Dipsy's Workshop establishes two new distinctive game modes to the series — Classic and Timed.
  • All rounds last approximately five minutes and thirty seconds (≈ 5:30) regardless of the mode.

Sliceables

  • There are eight sliceable objects within the gameplay — seven of them grant points, while one them gives a negative result when hit.
    • Nevertheless, two unused objects are present in the game files as well — the Candy and the Bomb; they are the counterparts for the previously mentioned entities.
  • The sliceables are all references from Fruit Ninja — the teletubbies represent the Fruit (the Cyan one is the Freeze Banana), while Teletubby Custards are portrayed as the Bombs.
  • Performing a quick slice that hits three or more targets results in a combo slice, which awards the player additionally by one hundred (100) points multiplied by the number of targets cut in that brief moment.
    • The most common combos are triples, quadruples, and quintuples.
    • The Cyan Teletubby and Tubby Custard do not contribute to combo slices.
  • If the one who controls Lake Dipsy manages to cut an object that was launched before the “Go” message at the beginning of every round a little before the text disappears, they will not receive anything from the hit — it cancels point earnings, freeze effects, and any negative outcomes for destroying a bowl of custard.

Teletubbies

  • Bearing in mind that seven teletubbies exist in Dipsy's Workshop, five of the them were already introduced in the past installments — the White Teletubby, Tinky Winky, Dipsy, Laa-Laa, and Po, at the same time, two new were brought in to the saga — Brown Teletubby and Cyan Teletubby.
  • None of the tubbies feature antennas, however, their abdominal screens remain — all while stuck in A-pose.
  • It is important to note that all of them use the textures of the White Tubby, which are recolored respectively for every other entity and reclassified as shaders.
  • Tinky Winky, Dipsy, Po, the Brown Tubby, and the Cyan Tubby had their faces forcefully “painted by hand” — producing them to seemingly have some kind of a “mask” — around their eyes and mouth, Caucasian skin tone is visible.
  • Noticeable, there are three pairs of two that contribute with the same amount of points:
    1. Primary — the White Tubby and the Brown Tubby — One Hundred Fifty (150) Points
    2. Secondary — Tinky Winky and Po — One Hundred Twenty-Five (125) Points
    3. Tertiary — Dipsy and Laa-Laa — One Hundred (100) Points
  • The Cyan Tubby is the only teletubby who does not award the player with points — the freeze is their only attribute.
  • The two brand-new figures could actually be the Cave Tubby and the Yeti Tubby.
    • Nonetheless, they may just be inspirations or all of this might be a coincidence.
  • The Cyan Teletubby's freeze lasts for six (6) seconds — during its time, it slows down the gameplay by a half (½) of its speed.
    • The slow adds extra three (3) seconds to the clock in Timed Mode.
  • All of the characters in the game files are referred to as “candy” — the Cyan Tubby differs from this solely in the sense of being named “candy freeze”.

Tubby Custard

  • Tubby Custards are the sole danger of the entire game.
  • Whenever launched, an audio file plays to alert the player.
  • It is the only entity to have variable outcomes once destroyed, which is purely conditioned by the chosen game mode.
    1. Classic Mode — Automatic Game Over
    2. Timed Mode — Negative Five Hundred (−500) Points
  • Impressively, bowls of Teletubby Custard used to be even bigger before the first update (V1.5) — defining them to be a much more serious threat than now.
  • In the game files, Tubby Custard is listed as a “candy bomb”.
